How it works

Booking for a group is simple.

1. Pick your dates

Use the calendar above to see all ten pods side by side for your dates. No price appears until you choose your nights, then you see the exact total for each pod.

2. Match pods to people

Three pod styles sleep 4 or 5 each. Mix and match until everyone has a bed. Every pod has its own hot tub, kitchen and fire pit, so nobody draws the short straw.

3. Book each pod

Book the pods you want one after another, each in a quick secure checkout. Rather sort it in one call? Phone us and we will hold the pods while we talk it through.
