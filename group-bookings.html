How it works

Booking for a group is simple.

1. Pick your dates

Use the whole calendar above to see every pod side by side for your dates, including our three couples’ pods at Broad Fen. No price appears until you choose your nights, then you see the exact total for each pod.

2. Match pods to people

Three pod styles sleep 4 or 5 each. Mix and match until everyone has a bed. Every pod has its own hot tub, kitchen and fire pit, so nobody draws the short straw.

3. Book them your way

Book pods one after another in quick secure checkouts, or tick the pods you want and send one request. We set it up as a single booking with one payment link, usually the same day.
