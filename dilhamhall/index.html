Our history

Five generations on the same land.

The Paterson family have farmed at Dilham Hall since 1949. Luke, a fifth‑generation farmer, and his wife Louise grew up knowing this place season by season, and opened it up so that others could slow down here too.

Today it is still a working farm, and one independently assessed as carbon negative. Our crops feed an anaerobic digestion plant that makes enough green energy for around 1,250 homes, and what comes out goes back on the fields as natural fertiliser. Alongside it sit the woodland and fen of a 95‑acre nature reserve and our own stretch of canal.
