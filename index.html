Good to know

Questions, answered.

Which retreat is right for us?

Broad Fen is our adults-only couples retreat, with three pods set in a 95 acre nature reserve. Tonnage Bridge is our luxury waterside glamping, ten pods on a private canal where families and dogs are welcome. Canal Camping is traditional camping and bell tents beside the water, ideal for families and groups.

Are dogs allowed?

Dogs are welcome at Tonnage Bridge (one dog per pod, £20 per stay) and at Canal Camping (£5 per dog per stay). Broad Fen is a dog-free retreat.

Are children welcome?

Children are very welcome at Tonnage Bridge and Canal Camping. Broad Fen is adults only, for guests aged 18 and over.

Do the pods have hot tubs?

Yes. Every pod at Broad Fen and Tonnage Bridge has its own private wood-fired, chemical-free hot tub. Canal Camping does not have hot tubs.

How many people does each pod sleep?

Broad Fen pods sleep 2 in a king bed. Tonnage Bridge pods sleep 4 or 5 depending on the layout. King and Twin sleeps 4 across two rooms, King, Single and Bunk sleeps 5 across two rooms, and the open-plan King and Bunk sleeps 4.

When will I see a price?

As soon as you choose your dates. Prices vary by season, length of stay and day of the week, so rather than showing a from price we show the exact total for your nights before you book.

Is there a minimum stay?

Broad Fen and Tonnage Bridge stays are a minimum of 2 nights, rising to 3 nights at Tonnage Bridge in peak season. Canal Camping is a minimum of 3 nights, or 2 nights off peak.

How much is the deposit?

A 50% deposit is paid when you book, and the balance is due six weeks before your stay. Full details are in our booking terms.

Can I hire a canoe without staying overnight?

Yes. Canoes, kayaks and paddleboards are available to day visitors as well as guests, with morning and afternoon sessions bookable online. You can also launch your own vessel for £10 per vessel per day.
